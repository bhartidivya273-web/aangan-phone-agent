# Aangan Studio - Pricing guidance (public version)

> **The internal pricing guide is not published in this repository.** It is supplied privately to the running system: either as the
> environment variable `PRICING_GUIDE_MD`, or as a local, git-ignored file `context/pricing.private.md`. It is used only by the
> classifier (to judge whether a budget a caller volunteers is far below the scope) and is never given to the voice agent.
> Pricing varies by site, materials and complexity. No number is ever quoted to a client; the designer confirms pricing at the consultation.

## What the agent must never say

- Any specific number, range, or per-sq-ft figure
- "It'll cost around..."
- "Our rates start at..."
- "For a 2BHK it's typically..."

**The only acceptable response to a pricing question**: "Pricing depends on the site, the materials you choose, and the scope — your designer will walk you through it in detail at the consultation. I can book that for you right now if you'd like."
